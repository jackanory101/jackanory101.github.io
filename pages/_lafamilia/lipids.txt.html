How Doctors Diagnosed Strokes Historically

  • Clinical Presentation: If a patient suddenly lost neurological function—such as losing the ability to speak, experiencing sudden paralysis on one side of the body, or rapidly slipping into unconsciousness—physicians relied on physical signs and the rapid onset of symptoms to diagnose a acute cerebrovascular event (stroke).
