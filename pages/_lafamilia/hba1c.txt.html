Understanding Your HbA1c Results An HbA1c test (glycated hemoglobin) measures the percentage of your red blood cells that have glucose coated onto them. Because red blood cells live for about three months, this test provides a reliable average of your blood sugar levels over the preceding 8 to 12 weeks, rather than just a single snapshot in time.
