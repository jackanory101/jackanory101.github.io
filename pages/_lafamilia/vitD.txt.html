Why Vitamin D Matters

  • Calcium Homeostasis & Bone Health: Vitamin D acts as a hormone that regulates how your intestines absorb calcium and phosphorus, which are essential for maintaining strong bone density and preventing conditions like osteomalacia or osteoporosis.
  • Immune & Cellular Function: Receptors for vitamin D are found across nearly every tissue in the body, where it plays a supportive role in immune system regulation, neuromuscular function, and cellular health.
