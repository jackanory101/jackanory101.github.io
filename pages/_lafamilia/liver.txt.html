Analysis

Understanding Your Liver Function Tests (LFTs) Liver function tests are a routine panel used to assess the overall health of your liver, screen for inflammation or damage, check how well the liver is processing proteins and clearing waste products, and monitor potential side effects from medications.

What Doctors Look For and Potential Concerns Outside Normal Ranges

  • Cellular Integrity (AST and ALT): Elevations in these enzymes typically signal irritation or damage to liver cells (hepatocytes), which can be triggered by factors like fatty liver changes, medications, alcohol, or viral inflammation. Your entirely normal enzymes show no indication of this.
  • Biliary Flow (ALP and GGT): Elevated levels can point toward bile duct obstruction, gallstone issues, or localized pressure on the biliary system. Your results confirm clear, unobstructed pathways.
  • Synthetic Function (Albumin and Bilirubin): If albumin drops or bilirubin climbs significantly, it can suggest the liver is struggling to manufacture essential proteins or clear metabolic waste. Your levels demonstrate that your liver’s workload handling is operating smoothly.

Your liver function test results are completely normal, showing healthy enzyme levels, stable protein synthesis, and no signs of inflammation or strain on the liver.
